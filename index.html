Driving research, education and collaboration in sustainability.

IITB Research Hub for Green Energy and Sustainability (IHCFSI) is an innovative hub focused on tackling the global climate crisis. We promote research in green energy and sustainability, facilitating technological, management, and policy solutions. IHCFSI connects academic research with practical applications while supporting education and entrepreneurship in these vital areas. Our mission is to cultivate the next generation of leaders in green energy and sustainability, empowering them to address complex environmental challenges through creativity and innovation.

Driving research, education and collaboration in sustainability.

IITB Research Hub for Green Energy and Sustainability (IHCFSI) is an innovative hub focused on tackling the global climate crisis. We promote research in green energy and sustainability, facilitating technological, management, and policy solutions. IHCFSI connects academic research with practical applications while supporting education and entrepreneurship in these vital areas. Our mission is to cultivate the next generation of leaders in green energy and sustainability, empowering them to address complex environmental challenges through creativity and innovation.

Director's Note

Prof. Shireesh Kedare, Director, Indian Institute of Technology Bombay

Green energy and sustainability are the cornerstones of progress in the 21st century. As the world grapples with climate change and environmental degradation, the need for innovative solutions has never been more critical. Today, India stands at a pivotal point in its journey towards sustainable development. This vision aligns with the India Green Energy Mission, which aims to create a robust ecosystem for renewable energy and sustainability ecosystem and net zero emission target of 2070. IIT Bombay is a key player in this mission, contributing from policy development to practical implementation.

IIT Bombay established the Research Hub for Green Energy and Sustainability Hub (IHCFSI) to integrate key resources and talent across all disciplines related to green energy and sustainability. IHCFSI aims to build a platform that propels India's ambitions towards global leadership in these critical areas. The vision of IHCFSI is to support the transition to green energy (environment-friendly energy) and sustainability. IHCFSI is poised to deliver transformational changes in the way research, training, incubation, management and policy development are conducted in the field of green energy and sustainability.

About Us

Core Learning Areas

Every learner builds a strong foundation through three essential core courses before choosing their electives.

  • Sustainability & Sustainable Development

  • Natural Resources & Ecology

  • Sustainable Lifestyle

Electives

Choose 2 of 12 electives that align with your career and interests. Courses are designed by IIT Bombay faculty and industry practitioners.

  • Regular workshops, seminars, and conferences to facilitate knowledge exchange

  • Collaborative research opportunities with leading global universities and corporations

  • Industry-tailored educational programs to prepare the next generation of sustainability leaders

  • A nurturing environment for green entrepreneurship and start-ups

  • State-of-the-art laboratory facilities equipped with the latest technologies

Whether you're a researcher looking to make a difference, an industry partner seeking sustainable solutions, or a student passionate about environmental issues, the Green Energy and Sustainability Research Hub offers a platform to turn your green aspirations into reality.

The hub was established in 2023 by an unprecedented 18.6 million USD donation from a US-based alumnus, and our hub represents a significant leap forward in environmental stewardship and sustainable solutions.

Knowledge Without Barriers

The initiative removes financial barriers through fully sponsored learning opportunities, empowering learners to apply sustainability knowledge wherever they live, learn, and work.

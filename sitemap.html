About Us

The Green Energy and Sustainability Research Hub at IIT Bombay is a pioneering centre dedicated to addressing the global climate crisis by facilitating technological, management & policy solutions through research in green energy and sustainability domains. We envision bridging the gap between academic research and real-world application while fostering education and entrepreneurship in these critical fields. We aim to nurture the next generation of green energy and sustainability leaders to tackle complex environmental challenges from multiple angles, promoting creativity and innovation. IHCFSI will be the first point of contact for green energy and sustainability research, education and other services at IITB.

Apart from research, we strive to provide:

  • Regular workshops, seminars, and conferences to facilitate knowledge exchange

  • Collaborative research opportunities with leading global universities and corporations

  • Industry-tailored educational programs to prepare the next generation of sustainability leaders

  • A nurturing environment for green entrepreneurship and start-ups

  • State-of-the-art laboratory facilities equipped with the latest technologies

Whether you're a researcher looking to make a difference, an industry partner seeking sustainable solutions, or a student passionate about environmental issues, the Green Energy and Sustainability Research Hub offers a platform to turn your green aspirations into reality.

The hub was established in 2023 by an unprecedented 18.6 million USD donation from a US-based alumnus, and our hub represents a significant leap forward in environmental stewardship and sustainable solutions.

Vision

To catalyse global leadership in sustainability & green energy through cutting-edge research, innovation and education by collaborating with academia, industry and regulators to drive the transition to a greener future.

Mission

  • To collaborate with Industries to carry out R&D in the area of green energy & sustainability to accelerate sustainability in industries and also support their net zero targets
  • Support in Fundamental & Translational Research in the area of green energy and sustainability
  • Creating a talent pool in the area of green energy and sustainability through education, training and workshop
  • To initiate collaborations with global universities - virtual webinars, guest lectures, joint academic programs and collaborative research projects.
  • To support Entrepreneurship in the area of green energy and sustainability
  • To set up Research Infrastructure at IITB

About the Center

The IIT Bombay Honeywell Centre of Excellence for Future Skills & Innovation (IHCFSI) is a state-of-the-art facility dedicated to advancing sustainable engineering, clean energy transition, and industry-ready technological skills.

"Empowering the next generation of engineers with practical digital twins and renewable energy skills to transform global industries."
